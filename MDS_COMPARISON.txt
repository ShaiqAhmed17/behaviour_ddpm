================================================================================
WHY NON-METRIC MDS WORKS BETTER
================================================================================

METRIC MDS:
- Tries to preserve: exact pairwise distances from 36D → 2D
- Problem: With 33 noisy features, distances become contradictory
- Each noisy feature adds independent noise to Euclidean distance
- In high-D space, noise swamps signal
- Result: Stress = 1030 (can't fit contradictory distances in 2D)

NON-METRIC MDS:
- Tries to preserve: rank order of distances (nearest ≈ nearest, farthest ≈ farthest)
- Uses monotonic regression instead of exact distance fitting
- Forgiving of noisy features as long as relative ordering is preserved
- The 33 noisy features don't corrupt ordinal relationships much
- Result: Stress = 0.27 (ordinal relationships are coherent despite noise)

ANALOGY:
- Metric MDS: tries to arrange dots so their exact distances match the distance matrix
- Non-metric MDS: tries to arrange dots so nearest neighbors stay near (ordinal)
- Ordinal relationships are more robust to noise than exact distances
